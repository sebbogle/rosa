Privacy

Rosa - updated 24 October 2026

Rosa is a recipe app by Sebastian Bogle. It runs in your browser, with no account and no server.

  • I collect nothing: no analytics, no tracking.
  • If you connect Google Drive, Rosa can access only the rosa folder it creates, plus your email address so it can show which account is connected.
  • Rosa uses this only to back up your recipes and sync them between your devices. Your data goes straight from your browser to Google. It never reaches me and is never shared.
  • Your email and a one-hour access token are stored only in your browser.
  • To delete everything: revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, delete the rosa folder in Drive, and clear Rosa's site data in your browser.
  • Rosa is provided as is, without warranty.
